Laptop keyboard repair

Dead keys, a spilled drink or letters that stick. Scott fits a new keyboard and brings it back.

When to call

  • Some keys do nothing
  • You spilled coffee, tea or soda on it
  • Keys stick or type the wrong letter
  • The keys are worn smooth

Spilled something

Shut the laptop down right away. Turn it upside down on a towel. Do not charge it. Then call. Fast action saves more than the keyboard.

How it goes

Scott picks up the laptop, checks for damage underneath, fits the new keyboard and tests every key. Then you schedule the return and pay when it is back.

A free quote by phone comes first. Have the laptop brand and model ready.
