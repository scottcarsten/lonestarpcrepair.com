How it works

Five simple steps from a computer problem to a computer that works.

Step 1. Call or email

Call (817) 904-4877 or send an email. Leave a message if we are on a job and we will call you back.

Step 2. Tell us what is wrong

Describe what the computer is doing. The brand and model help if you know them. No tech talk needed.

Step 3. Get a free quote

We give you a free quote before any work starts. No prices are posted on this site because every computer is different. If you like the quote, we go ahead.

Step 4. It gets fixed

We do the repair and call you if we find something that changes the plan. Your files are protected before any big step.

Step 5. You are back up and running

Your computer works again and we make sure you are happy with it.
