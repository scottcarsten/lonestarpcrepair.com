About Lone Star PC Repair

Hi, I am Scott. I have worked in IT for 28 years, and I am based in the Fort Worth area.

Why this shop

In 28 years I have seen plenty of files go bad at the worst time. A small office does not have an IT department to call. It has a person who is trying to get the books open before payroll.

That is who I built this for. I keep things plain, I tell you what I think before you commit, and I start with a free sample check so you are not guessing.

The work is data rescue for the mainstream databases and files, plus PowerShell and Microsoft 365 scripts for small offices.

How I work

  • I tell you what I see, in plain words.
  • You get a quote before work starts.
  • I work on a copy and leave your original alone.
  • Your files are handled privately and never shared. All client data is automatically deleted and securely wiped 30 days after delivery.
